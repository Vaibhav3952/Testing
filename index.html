01 — About

Designing for the details.

I'm a builder who lives at the intersection of design and engineering. I care about the things most people skip — the rhythm of a typeface, the easing on a hover, the moment a page first loads.

My recent work spans Indian travel-tech, conversational booking flows, and analytics dashboards. I work primarily in vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, reaching for Chart.js, Supabase, and lightweight backends when a project calls for them.

When I'm not shipping, I'm usually following the markets, on a tennis court, or chasing the next side project.
